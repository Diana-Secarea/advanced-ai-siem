CONNECT YOUR MACHINES

Monitor an endpoint.

The collector is a 13 MB background service that reads the machine's logs and ships them here. Nothing is analysed on the endpoint — no models, no Python. Detection, ML scoring and the AI analyst all run on this server, and the machine's events appear in Live Alerts within a minute of install.
