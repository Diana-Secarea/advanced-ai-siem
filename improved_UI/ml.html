DESIGN

Why two models, not one.

🌲

Isolation Forest

Isolates anomalies with random axis-aligned cuts — an extreme rule_level or failed_count is separated in a few steps. Fast, unsupervised, robust to noise. Weakness: misses subtle feature combinations.

🧠

Autoencoder

Learns to reconstruct normal alerts through a 16→8→4→8→16 bottleneck. Attacks reconstruct badly → high error. Catches unusual combinations even when no single feature is extreme.

🛡️

Adversarial robustness

The evasion strategies are contradictory: blending into density (fool IF) means being statistically average (easy for AE), and mimicking the manifold (fool AE) means common features (easy for IF). You can't beat both at once.

UNSUPERVISED BY DESIGN

No human ever labels an alert.

🌲 Isolation Forest — contamination='auto'

Fitted on all 2,171 alerts with no label filter. It makes no assumption about the proportion of anomalies — genuinely unsupervised.

🧠 Autoencoder — iterative trimming ×3

~30% attacks left in on purpose; train → drop top-25% reconstruction errors → drop 15% → final clean fit. Self-supervised bootstrapping, zero labels.
