How it works

From raw log to explained incident.

Collect

Wazuh agents stream every auth event, syscall and rule hit into the engine in real time.

Score

16 engineered features feed the IF + AE + UEBA ensemble; a learned stacker fuses them into one score.

Explain

The RAG copilot retrieves matching CVEs and intel from Qdrant and explains the incident with citations.

React

The Reactor triages, blocks, scans or emails — every action written to a tamper-evident ledger.
